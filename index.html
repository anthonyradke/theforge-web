01 · The Forge

Built for every
kind of strong.

Powerlifting. Bodybuilding. Powerbuilding. General fitness. One roof, one membership, no wrong reason to be here.

A forge is where raw material meets heat, pressure, and a hammer, and comes out the other side as something useful. That's the idea behind the whole place.

The Forge is being built so a competitive powerlifter can walk into a dedicated powerlifting section and find exactly the equipment they need, a bodybuilder has a real machine and cable selection to work with, and someone who just wants to lose their first twenty pounds never feels like they wandered into the middle of a meet.

Same room. Same welcome. Different weight on the bar.

  • A dedicated powerlifting section with competition-quality equipment
  • A full bodybuilding floor with machines, cables, dumbbells and free weights
  • 24/7 access for members
  • Open to every belief, every background, every starting point

02 · Our Purpose

Build more than muscle.

The Forge was created to help people grow physically, emotionally and spiritually. Strength is where it starts. It isn't where it ends.

  • I

    Physical

    Build your body through training.

    Your body is the temple you were given. We take care of it with barbells, machines, hard work and good coaching, and we take it seriously, whatever your starting point looks like.

  • II

    Emotional

    Build relationships through community.

    Iron sharpens iron. You'll get spotted, encouraged, and known by name. The friendships built between sets carry a lot further than the gym floor, and they hold you up on the weeks that are hard.

  • III

    Spiritual

    Build your faith through fellowship, prayer and God's Word.

    There's room here for faith, for prayer, for hard questions, for forgiveness, and for guidance. Not as a program you sign up for, just as a normal part of how this place operates.

You don't have to be a Christian to train here. You don't have to believe what we believe. Everyone who walks through our doors will be welcomed and respected.

The Forge is a temple of strength and faith.

03 · Our Story

Ten years under the bar,
and a calling to build this.

  1. The Training

    Ten-plus years of his own reps

    Jonathan Walker has spent more than a decade under a barbell, long enough to learn what actually works, what only sounds good online, and what it takes to keep showing up when nothing is going right.

  2. The Competition

    Two sports, two titles

    He competed in both bodybuilding and powerlifting, and won at both, taking his local bodybuilding show, and going on to win the IPL Olympia on the platform. That's why The Forge refuses to pick a side between the two. He's lived in both worlds.

  3. The Coaching

    Three years in someone else's corner

    Coaching changed the goal. Hitting your own numbers is satisfying; watching someone else discover what they're capable of is a different thing entirely. That's the part that stuck.

  4. The Calling

    Strength of body and of spirit

    The Forge exists because Jonathan believes helping people become stronger, physically and spiritually, is what God has called him to do. Not a business plan that got a verse attached to it afterward. The reason the doors are opening at all.

  5. Early 2027

    The Forge opens in Piedmont

    Roughly 9,000 square feet in the Greenville area, being built right now. Come find out what you're made of.

09 · Questions

Straight answers

Where exactly will The Forge be?

Piedmont, South Carolina, in the Greenville area, roughly 9,000 square feet. The exact address goes out as soon as the lease is final. Join the list and you'll get it the day we can share it.

When do you open?

We're targeting early 2027. Build-out timelines move, so we'll keep the list updated as things firm up rather than promising a date we can't hold.

Is this only for powerlifters?

Not at all. The Forge is a powerbuilding gym. There's a dedicated powerlifting section for people who compete or train like they do, and a full bodybuilding floor of machines, cables and dumbbells for everyone else. If your goal is to look better, feel better, or just move well at sixty, you're in the right place.

I've never really trained before. Will I be out of place?

No. A serious gym and a welcoming gym aren't opposites, and The Forge is being built to be both. Our coaches can teach you the lifts if you want the help, and nobody here has forgotten what their first week felt like.

Do I have to be a Christian to train here?

No. Faith is why the owner is building this place and it shapes how people get treated here, but The Forge is open to everyone. You'll never be pressured or made to feel like an outsider. You'll be welcomed, coached and respected the same as anyone else.

What will membership cost?

Standard membership is $65/month on a 12-month agreement. Flex is $85/month, month to month. The first 50 members get the Founding rate of $55/month, locked in for life while the membership stays active. Every plan has a $25 one-time enrollment fee and full 24/7 access. Day passes are $15 and week passes $40.

Do you offer discounts?

Yes, for eligible students, military members, veterans, first responders, teachers and healthcare workers. We also offer family membership packages. Contact us and we'll walk you through the options.

Will there be personal training?

Yes. We offer one-on-one personal training as well as specialized powerlifting and bodybuilding coaching. Ask us about training and coaching packages. And if you're a coach looking to train clients here, we'd like to hear from you.

What are the hours?

Members get 24/7 access. Staffed hours will be announced before opening.
