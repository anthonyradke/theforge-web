01 — The Forge

This isn't a fitness center.
It's a workshop for people.

A forge is where raw material meets heat, pressure, and a hammer — and comes out the other side as something useful. That's the whole idea.

The Forge Strength Club is a members-first strength gym built around barbells, real coaching, and a standard of behavior that makes the room worth walking into. No mirrors-only vanity floor. No contracts designed to trap you. No music you have to shout over.

We're built for the lifter chasing a 500 lb deadlift and for the 52-year-old who hasn't touched a barbell in thirty years. Same room. Same standard. Different weight on the bar.

  • Competition-grade platforms, racks, and calibrated plates
  • Coaching that starts with your first session, not your first upsell
  • Month-to-month membership — cancel any time, no exit fee
  • Open to every belief, every background, every starting point

02 — Mission & Values

Why we're opening the doors

Our mission is to build strong people — physically, mentally, and spiritually — and to make Greenville better one member at a time. The barbell is just the tool we use to do it.

  • I

    Faith

    The cross is in our logo because it's in our foundation. We open the doors with prayer and we mean it. And you are just as welcome here if you don't believe a word of it — nobody gets recruited, cornered, or asked to sign anything.

  • II

    Strength

    Strength is measurable, honest, and impossible to fake. It carries into everything else: your work, your patience, your family, the way you handle a bad week. We train it on purpose.

  • III

    Community

    Iron sharpens iron. You'll get spotted, corrected, encouraged, and occasionally talked out of a stupid third attempt. You'll do the same for someone else. That's the trade.

  • IV

    Discipline

    Motivation is weather. Discipline is climate. We build systems, programs, and habits that keep working on the days you'd rather stay in the truck.

03 — Our Story

It started in a garage
with two people and a bad bar.

  1. The Garage

    One rack, one radio, one standard

    Before there was a name, there was a two-car garage, a rusted 45 lb bar, and a handful of guys meeting at 5 a.m. because that was the only hour nobody could take from us. No heat in January. No air in July. Nobody missed.

  2. The Problem

    Greenville deserved better

    Every gym we tried was one of two things: a big-box club that sold you a year and hoped you'd never come, or a hardcore room where a beginner got stared at until they quit. There was no place that took the training seriously and took people seriously.

  3. The Decision

    Build the room we wanted to train in

    So we stopped complaining and started drawing floor plans on napkins. Equipment lists. Coaching standards. A code of conduct that fits on one page. The kind of gym where your kid could walk in and you'd be glad about who they met.

  4. 2026

    The Forge opens

    Doors open in Greenville this year. The bar is better now. The standard hasn't moved. Come find out what you're made of.

04 — Training

Four ways to train here

Whether you want a coach in your ear three days a week or you just want a clean platform and to be left alone, there's a lane for you.

  • Open Gym

    Full floor access, 24/7 for members. Run your own program, use our equipment, and get a form check any time a coach is on the floor.

    Included with every membership

  • Group Strength

    Coach-led small groups capped at 12. Squat, press, pull, carry — programmed in cycles so you're always working toward a number, not just getting tired.

    Mon / Wed / Fri · 5:30a, 12p, 5:30p

  • 1-on-1 Coaching

    Private sessions built around your body, your history, and your goal — whether that's a first pull-up, a first meet, or getting off blood pressure medication.

    By appointment · packages available

  • Barbell Club

    Competitive powerlifting and weightlifting. Meet prep, attempt selection, handling on the platform, and a team that travels with you.

    Coach approval · add-on to any plan

08 — Questions

Straight answers

I've never touched a barbell. Am I going to be out of place?

No. About half the people who walk in have never squatted with a bar. Every membership starts with a free onboarding session where a coach teaches you the lifts, sets your starting weights, and shows you how the room works. Nobody here got strong by accident, and nobody forgets what day one felt like.

Do I have to be a Christian to train here?

Not even a little. Faith is why the founders built this place and it shapes how we treat people — but The Forge is open to everyone. You will never be pressured, recruited, or made to feel like an outsider. You'll get coached, spotted, and respected the same as anyone else.

When do you open?

We're targeting [Month] 2026 in Greenville. Get on the founding member list and you'll be the first to know the address, the opening date, and the walkthrough schedule — plus you'll lock the founding rate before public pricing goes live.

Is there a contract?

All plans are month-to-month. No initiation fee, no annual maintenance fee, no cancellation fee. Cancel from your phone in about thirty seconds. We'd rather earn next month than trap you in it.

What are the hours?

Members get 24/7 fob access. Coaches are on the floor during staffed hours (see below), and classes run morning, midday, and evening on Monday, Wednesday, and Friday.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes — there's a supervised kids' area during staffed hours, and teens 14+ can train on the floor after completing a coached onboarding with a parent present.

I'm traveling through Greenville. Can I drop in?

Always. Day passes are $20, and they're free for lifters visiting from out of town — just tell us where you train. Iron sharpens iron, and that includes yours.
